# City catalog attribution

Place data for Argentina, Uruguai, Paraguai, Peru, Bolívia, Chile, Colômbia, Venezuela, Equador, Guiana, Suriname, Guiana Francesa, Panamá, Costa Rica, Honduras, El Salvador, Guatemala, Belize, México, Estados Unidos, Canadá are derived from GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0).

Runtime catalogs keep administrative seats and populated places above 1,000 inhabitants; neighbourhoods, tiny localities, farm/religious villages, historical, abandoned and destroyed places are excluded.
